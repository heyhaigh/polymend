What to do when your slicer says a model has errors

A made-up article on a made-up site, to show the embed in place.

Everything inside the frame below is Polymend, loaded from a different address than this page, exactly as it would be on a real site. The surrounding page is deliberately a different design.

You download a figure, drop it into your slicer, and a yellow warning tells you the model has open edges or non-manifold edges. The model looks fine. The slicer disagrees.

Most of the time the cause is small: a few stray triangles stuck to the surface, or a pinhole you would never see. You can fix those right here without installing anything. Drop your file on the box below.

What just happened

The tool above ran in your browser. Your file did not go anywhere. It removed the stray pieces, patched the small holes, and handed you a file your slicer should take without complaint.

If it says "Partly repaired", the damage is bigger than a quick fix, and it is time to open a 3D editor.
